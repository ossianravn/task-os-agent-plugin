# Ways of working

<When communication documents are mapped:> How to write to each reader is in <`path`, `path`>. Read them before writing to anyone, and run their checklists before you hand over a draft.

## Defaults
- Draft first: anything addressed to someone else stays a draft until the person sends it. Messages wait in `work/drafts/`; deliverables go in <the deliverable folder, named the way it names things, or `work/drafts/`>.
- Check before it goes out: run the Task OS `verify` skill on every draft. A draft is not ready while a statement phrased as fact rests on a source that cannot settle it.
- Keep the person's own text: when checking or editing their draft, keep its structure, order and wording. Suggest line edits, and offer additions separately.
- Write for the reader: start from what they already know and what they do not (`knows` and `does_not_know` in their person record).
- Name the source of every number in text someone else will read, and what that source cannot show when it limits the number.
- Ask first: any change outside this folder, such as moving a card, editing a ticket, booking a meeting or submitting a form in an app, waits for the person's OK. An OK covers the change it was given for; ask again for the next one.
- Never send: <what setup recorded, such as user-level data or screenshots that show customer data>.
