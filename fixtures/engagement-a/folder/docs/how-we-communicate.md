# How we communicate with Acme

## Karen (Head of Digital)
- Weekly status mail on Fridays, in Danish, at most ten lines.
- Lead with what changed and what she needs to decide. Explain any GA4 term in plain words.
- Numbers go out only after I have checked them against the source. Say where each number comes from.

## Jonas (lead developer)
- Teams chat for quick questions; Jira comments for anything that changes a ticket.
- English is fine.

## Mette (marketing analyst)
- Email. She sends the weekly order report from the shop backend on Mondays.
- She gets dashboard links, not raw exports.

## Never
- Nothing goes to Karen or anyone outside the web team without my review.
- No screenshots that show customer data.
